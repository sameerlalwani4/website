01 — About

The engineer who thinks
in products.

I've spent the last 4+ years as a Software Engineer at Metron Security, building integrations for enterprise platforms like Azure, Okta, Cisco Duo, AWS, and Proofpoint. But my favourite moments were never just about writing code — they were about sitting in requirement sessions, shaping backlogs, and watching a feature go from a customer pain point to a working solution.

That itch to own the "why" behind the product led me to launch two startups — SundarYatra (hyperlocal homestays) and ForeverBlush (campus dating app) — where I wore the PM hat end-to-end: user research, MVP scoping, growth experiments, and partnerships.

Now I'm formalising that strategic thinking with an Executive MBA (Leadership & Strategy) at NMIMS. I'm ready to bring my technical depth and customer obsession to a Product role — and build things that genuinely matter.
